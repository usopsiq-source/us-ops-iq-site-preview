08 — Questions

Straight answers.

Why pay instead of getting a free look?

A real review takes real work, so the assessment is the product, not a sales pitch. You get a written report, a walkthrough and a trial.

Are you selling software?

No. We start with how the work gets done. The report can recommend tools, software or simple process changes, and any implementation is a separate project you can decline.

How do I know it won’t be generic advice?

It is built from your intake, your call and two of your real processes. The “Hours we found” table shows the tasks, time and frequency behind every number, so you can check our reasoning.

I’ve tried software before and it didn’t stick.

That is common. We look at the process before the tool, suggest changes that fit what you already use, and name a fallback if a fix breaks.

Do you need access to my systems?

You choose what to show us, and you should hide private information. We don’t ask for passwords. Any later access uses accounts you own and can turn off.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

No. What we stand behind is above: a report built from your real work, with at least five hours a week of identified opportunity. If you don’t feel you got the value promised, talk with Steward and he will make it right.

What happens after call two?

We offer the trial. You can act on the plan yourself or ask for a fixed-price implementation proposal. There is no required next step.
