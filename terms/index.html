Terms of the US Ops IQ Assessment

What you buy

The US Ops IQ Assessment is $999. It includes a review of two real processes using your intake and a 15-20 minute call, a written report with 3-5 ranked recommendations, an “Hours we found” table, a 30-minute walkthrough and one free 14-day Quick Win Trial. You keep the report whether or not you hire us to implement anything.

Timing

Your report arrives within five business days of the call.

Our promise

Your report will identify at least five hours a week of time-saving opportunity, documented in the “Hours we found” table, with 3-5 recommendations. If you don’t feel you got the value promised, Steward will talk with you and make it right. We do not offer a money-back guarantee. Time and dollar figures are estimates; actual results depend on how the changes are carried out.

Quick Win Trial

One trial per business, free, with no card on file. Setup within five business days of your yes, up to two hours of setup, a check-in on day 7 and a 14-day run. Services only reply to people who already contacted you. Access stays in accounts you own. There is no automatic paid continuation: at the end you keep it at an agreed price or we switch it off and hand over the settings at no charge.

Implementation credit

The $999 fee is credited in full toward implementation if you sign within 14 days of the walkthrough. Minimum implementation project: $1,500. Credit does not apply to retainers. Implementation is a separate, optional project.

Your information

Do not send passwords, payment details or private customer, tenant or guest information. See the privacy notice.
